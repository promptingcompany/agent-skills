# Report in the client's benchmark language

Read the spec end to end first, map every task to its evals, and report in its shapes: its success measures, its evidence labels, its reporting rules. Answer its open questions where the run can.

**Why:** A report in your own terms has to be translated before the client can use it, and the translation is where trust leaks. In their terms it drops straight into their process.

**Pattern:**
- Save a local copy of the spec, including collapsed sections and sub-pages.
- One mapping table: task, spec eval, same, different.
- A section listing the spec's open questions this run answered, and how.

**Example:** The spec's findings log had the incoming-timestamp question as 'confirmed by relay'. The run verified it on 32 delivered messages.
